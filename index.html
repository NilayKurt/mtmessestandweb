Exhibition Stand & Trade Show Booth Solutions

Choose the right stand type for your trade fair success

Custom Wooden Stands

Full custom builds — curved walls, integrated LED screens, suspended ceilings, branded floor finishes. We work from your 3D files or design from scratch. Typical project: 18–100m², 3–6 weeks from approval to opening day.

Maxima Modular Stands

Aluminium frame system with fabric graphics, backlit panels, and integrated shelving. Looks premium, costs 30–40% less than wood. Ideal for companies doing multiple shows per year who want a reusable, reconfigurable setup.

Octanorm Modular Stands

The industry standard modular system — clean lines, fast assembly, easy to transport. We stock Octanorm profiles in white, silver, and black. Digital print panels mounted on magnetic rails for quick branding changes between shows.

Enhancing Package Stands

Already bought a package stand from the fair organizer? We overlay it with printed graphics, add furniture, lighting, and display units. In two days, a generic shell becomes your branded space — at a fraction of a custom build.

Country Pavilion Solutions

National pavilions, trade ministry stands, group exhibits. We handle the entire site: individual kiosks, shared meeting areas, storage, signage, and hostess coordination. Single contract, single point of contact.

Turn-key Exhibitor Services

Beyond the stand itself: furniture rental (chairs, sofas, counters), professional hostesses and interpreters, in-booth catering, brochure printing, hotel bookings, airport shuttles. One invoice, one supplier.

Why MT Messe Stand?

Four reasons exhibitors choose us for international trade fairs

Picking an exhibition stand builder in a country you do not live in is a leap of faith. You send a brief and a budget, then fly in hoping the stand is ready. We have been on the receiving end of that trust since 2010. Here is what makes it work.

No Subcontractors

We own our factory and workshop in Istanbul. Every stand is built in-house — no third-party shortcuts, no quality surprises. When you visit during production, you will see your actual stand taking shape, not a photo from another company's portfolio.

On-Time, Every Time

Your stand is ready before the fair opens. Guaranteed. We plan backwards from your event date and never miss a deadline. In fifteen years, we have never left a client standing in an empty booth space on opening morning.

Dedicated Assistant

Every project comes with a Junior Assistant-Mate — your single point of contact on WhatsApp, before and during the fair. Need an extra chair at 8pm? A last-minute print? A restaurant recommendation for your client dinner? One message, done.

15 Years of Trust
