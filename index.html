How We Work

Four steps to your perfect exhibition stand

1. Discovery & Quote

Share your fair details, m², stand type and budget. We send a tailored proposal within 48 hours.

2. 3D Design & Approval

Our designers create a custom 3D render. You review, request changes, and approve.

3. Production & Logistics

We manufacture in our Istanbul factory, handle shipping, customs, and transport to the fair venue.

4. Installation & Support

Our team sets up your stand. A dedicated Junior Assistant-Mate stays with you throughout the event.
