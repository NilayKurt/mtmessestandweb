Stand, Booth & Expo Solutions

Six stand types, one production team. From an 18m² booth to an 800m² country pavilion — every project follows the same build standard. Custom wooden booths for brands that want full creative control, Maxima modular systems for teams that exhibit at multiple fairs per year, enhancing packages that upgrade a shell scheme in 48 hours, turn-key exhibitor services that handle everything beyond the booth itself, and organiser solutions for bulk stand programmes across venues. Tell us your fair, your footprint, and your budget — we will recommend the right build.

Custom Wooden Stands

The trade show booth that makes your brand unforgettable.

Full custom builds — curved walls, integrated LED screens, suspended ceilings, branded floor finishes. We work from your 3D exhibition stand design files or create everything from scratch. Typical project: 18–800m², 3–6 weeks from approval to opening day. Every custom exhibition stand is manufactured in our own workshops, built to specification, and delivered on time.

Maxima Modular Stands

Aluminium frame system with fabric graphics, backlit panels, and integrated shelving. New seamless fabric walls give you an uninterrupted, full-height print surface — no panel gaps, a finish much closer to a custom wooden build. Looks premium, costs 30–40% less than wood. Ideal for companies doing multiple exhibitions per year who want a reusable, reconfigurable modular booth setup.

Enhancing Package Stands

Already bought a package stand from the fair organizer? We overlay it with printed fascia board graphics, add furniture rental, lighting, and display units. In two days, a generic shell becomes your branded exhibition space — at a fraction of a custom build. Ideal upgrade for companies who want more presence without the full investment.

Country Pavilion Solutions

National pavilions, trade ministry stands, group exhibits — complete country pavilion design and construction. We handle the entire site: individual kiosks, shared meeting areas, storage, signage, and hostess coordination. Single contract, single point of contact. Built in the workshop closest to the fair venue.

Turn-key Exhibitor Services

Beyond the stand itself: trade show booth furniture rental (chairs, sofas, counters), professional hostesses and interpreters, in-booth catering, brochure printing, hotel bookings, airport shuttles. One invoice, one supplier. Every service managed by your dedicated project contact — no chasing multiple vendors.

Turn-key Organizer Services

For fair organizers: pre-built package stands, bulk furniture rental, lanyards, tote bags, trophies, and branded merchandise. We supply everything an organizer needs to kit out their exhibitor packages — single source, consistent quality, volume pricing. From 50 to 5,000 units.

How We Work

Four steps, one team, zero surprises. We have refined our process over 300+ builds across 15+ countries. It starts with a discovery call where we map your fair dates, venue rules, and budget. Then our design team delivers a 3D render within 5 business days. Production runs in the workshop closest to your event — Istanbul, Dubai, or Moscow — with a full dry-build quality check before crating. Installation is handled by our own crews who know the venue and its technical office. Throughout the process, your project manager is one WhatsApp message away.

1. Discovery & Quote

Share your expo details — venue, m², stand type and budget. We send a tailored proposal with 3D design scope and transparent pricing. No hidden costs.

2. 3D Design & Approval

Our designers create a custom trade show booth 3D rendering. You review, request changes, and approve. We don't start production until you're satisfied.

3. Production & Logistics

We manufacture in the workshop closest to your event — Istanbul, Dubai or Moscow. We handle shipping, customs, and transport to the fair venue. One team, start to finish.

4. Installation & Support

Our team sets up your booth before opening day. A dedicated project manager stays on standby throughout the fair. Dismantling, storage, or shipping to your next show — all arranged.
