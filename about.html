About MT Messe Stand

Your reliable partner for exhibition success in Turkey since 2010.

MT Messe Stand is a complete trade fair and exhibitor services provider located in Istanbul, Turkey. We offer turn-key stand solutions — from custom wooden designs to modular Octanorm and Maxima systems — with a full range of exhibitor services under one roof.

300+ stands built across 28+ countries. Our clients include companies from Japan, China, Germany, Austria, Italy, Spain, Bulgaria, and Thailand. We bring international standards to every project.
